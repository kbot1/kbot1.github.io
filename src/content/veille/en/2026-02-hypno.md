## Background

In resource-limited settings, access to linear accelerators is the main bottleneck. Fewer fractions means more patients treated. The trial, presented in the ASTRO 2023 plenary session, is now published.

## Design

792 patients with locally advanced head and neck squamous cell carcinoma, mostly not HPV-related, at 12 centres. Randomisation between:

- 55 Gy in 20 fractions, 5 per week, over 4 weeks;
- 66 Gy in 33 fractions, 6 per week, over 5.5 weeks.

Weekly cisplatin was added or not according to local practice. Two non-inferiority primary endpoints (10% margin): locoregional control and grade ≥3 late toxicity.

## Results

- Locoregional control: non-inferiority shown (p=0.04).
- Grade ≥3 late toxicity: non-inferiority shown (p=0.004).
- At 3 years, between-arm differences were at most 1.4 points for overall survival, progression-free survival, locoregional control and severe late toxicity.

## What it changes

This is the first phase 3 trial to validate a curative 20-fraction schedule in locally advanced head and neck cancer. It is aimed first at constrained health systems. Elsewhere, it provides solid support when a short course is needed (comorbidities, distance, overloaded departments), without replacing the standard schedule in fit patients.
