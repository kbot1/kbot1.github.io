## Background

Hypofractionation has become standard for breast-only irradiation. Many teams, however, kept 50 Gy in 25 fractions whenever the nodes were included, for fear of lymphoedema and plexopathy.

## Design

French UNICANCER trial at 29 centres. 1,265 patients needing nodal irradiation after complete resection, 1,221 in the per-protocol analysis. Randomisation between:

- 40 Gy in 15 fractions over 3 weeks;
- 50 Gy in 25 fractions over 5 weeks.

In both arms, the nodes and the breast or chest wall were irradiated. Primary endpoint: ipsilateral arm lymphoedema, with a non-inferiority margin set at an HR of 1.545.

## Results

- Median follow-up: 4.8 years.
- Lymphoedema in 25% of patients, with no difference between arms: HR 1.02 (95% CI 0.79 to 1.31). Non-inferiority was shown (p<0.001).
- 3-year cumulative incidence: 23.4% vs 22.2%.
- Grade ≥3 adverse events: 8% vs 13%.

## What it changes

Together with the Chinese postmastectomy data and the Danish Skagen 1 trial, HypoG-01 removes the last barrier to locoregional hypofractionation. The 15-fraction schedule becomes the standard, including nodal irradiation: two weeks less treatment for these patients. A quality-assurance analysis of the trial is a reminder that rigorous contouring and planning matter.
