## Background

Recent years have been eventful for cervical cancer: pembrolizumab with chemoradiotherapy (KEYNOTE-A18), induction chemotherapy (INTERLACE), surgical de-escalation. Up-to-date French guidelines were needed.

## Method

Guidelines produced under the auspices of the French national college of gynaecologists (CNGOF) by a multidisciplinary panel representing the relevant societies, radiation oncologists included. They rely on FIGO 2018 staging, the GRADE method and a two-round Delphi with 87 reviewers. 130 clinical questions led to 158 recommendations; for 71 questions, no recommendation could be made.

## Key points

Among the messages highlighted by the authors:

- image-guided brachytherapy after chemoradiotherapy;
- pembrolizumab in selected locally advanced or metastatic settings;
- no minimally invasive hysterectomy from stage IB1;
- risk-adapted surgical radicality;
- sentinel node biopsy with ultrastaging.

## What it changes

This becomes the national reference for harmonising practice and tumour boards. For radiation oncologists, it confirms image-guided adaptive brachytherapy as the standard after chemoradiotherapy and frames the arrival of immunotherapy. Only the points announced in the abstract are summarised here: the full graded recommendations are in the paper.
