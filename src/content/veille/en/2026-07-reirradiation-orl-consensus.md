## Background

Reirradiation for recurrent and second primary head and neck cancers is common, but practice varies widely between centres: patient selection, volumes, doses, cumulative constraints. Randomised trials remain rare.

## Design

This is not a trial but an expert consensus built through a formal agreement process. It is endorsed by the Reirradiation Collaborative Group, the ESTRO reirradiation focus group and ASTRO.

## Content

The recommendations cover the main steps of care:

- patient selection;
- imaging;
- target volume delineation;
- treatment planning;
- dose accumulation with the previous course;
- toxicity management.

## What it changes

The text provides a common framework where each team had its own rules. It should serve as a reference to harmonise practice and local protocols, document cumulative dose, and better select the patients who can truly benefit from curative reirradiation. Only the main lines are summarised here: the detailed recommendations are in the paper.
