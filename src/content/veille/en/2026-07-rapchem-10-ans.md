## Background

After neoadjuvant chemotherapy for cN1 breast cancer, should the nodes still be irradiated once they are sterilised? NSABP B-51 (2025) showed no benefit of nodal irradiation in ypN0 patients. RAPCHEM adds 10-year real-world follow-up.

## Design

Dutch prospective registry of 838 cT1-2N1 patients treated with primary chemotherapy. The intensity of locoregional radiotherapy was matched to post-treatment risk, defined by nodal response: 291 low-risk, 370 intermediate-risk and 177 high-risk patients.

## Results

- 10-year isolated locoregional recurrence: 2.9% for all patients.
- By group: 2.4% (low), 3.2% (intermediate) and 2.8% (high), with no significant difference.
- 10-year recurrence-free interval: 79.2%. Overall survival: 83.0%.

## What it changes

With 10 years of follow-up, RAPCHEM supports a response-guided de-escalation strategy consistent with B-51: locoregional recurrence stays low when irradiation is reduced in good responders. It is a registry, not a randomised trial, but it reinforces the role of response to neoadjuvant treatment in the nodal irradiation decision.
